# MVP Demo Safe Script

This script keeps the live staging demo read-only and avoids claims that are not supported by the current product state.

## Safe Demo Path

1. Open `https://staging.30sek24.com/expo-3d?salesDemo=1`
2. Show the sponsor boulevard, the lane cards, and the public CTA controls.
3. Optionally switch between the visible public expo modes.
4. Open `https://staging.30sek24.com/modular-homes/studio?homeStudio=1&homeDemo=1`
5. Click the `Design` tab.
6. Show that preset choices update the summary and selected state.
7. Keep the demo on the preset configurator and quote preview only.

## Do Not Click

- Do not pitch the booth profile as a live-room experience.
- Do not click submit on any quote form.
- Do not claim full custom-home detail editing.
- Do not rely on the protected admin follow-through path unless a fresh valid QA session has been prepared separately.

## Suggested Talk Track

- "This is the public expo sales path and the modular-home configurator preview."
- "The configurator supports a limited set of presets and updates the summary as you change them."
- "The controlled demo should stay on the public expo and modular-home studio surfaces."
